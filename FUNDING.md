## 🛡️ The Mission

In an era where everyday workflows and critical business tasks are increasingly routed through cloud-hosted large language models, sensitive data leaks are happening at an unprecedented scale. 

Vigil AI was built from the ground up to solve this challenge with an uncompromising philosophy:
- **100% Local-First:** All regex scanning, neural inference, and cryptographic operations execute directly on your device.
- **Zero Telemetry:** No tracking, no phone-home analytics, and no remote server dependencies.
- **100% Open-Source:** Fully auditable, transparent, and free under the MIT License.

---

## ☕ Why Your Support Matters

Vigil AI is developed and maintained independently by a solo developer. There are no venture capitalists, no hidden telemetry monetization, and no proprietary paid tiers.

Maintaining a multi-layered security ecosystem — spanning a high-performance WebExtension, a native Rust desktop companion daemon, and rigorous automated CI testing across Linux, macOS, and Windows — requires continuous dedication, hardware testing environments, and significant infrastructure maintenance.

Your financial support directly helps:
1. **Sustain Full-Time Maintenance & Rapid Security Response:** Keep the extension and companion compatible with rapidly evolving browser releases and AI platforms.
2. **Fund Continuous Testing & Hardware Infrastructure:** Support dedicated testbeds for cross-platform hardware enclave testing (TPM / Apple Secure Enclave) and CI compute.
3. **Keep Vigil Free & Independent for Everyone:** Ensure privacy tools remain accessible to students, journalists, researchers, and developers worldwide without paywalls or subscriptions.
